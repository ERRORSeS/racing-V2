# Race levels, requirements and progression

Starting → Maiden → Novice/Allowance → Open Stakes → Listed → G3 → G2 → G1. Claiming and Handicap are parallel routes. This is coaching guidance, not a mandatory unlock ladder. Suggestions refer to relevant current skills on the 0–100 scale; genetic caps, health and actual opponents still matter.

All normal entries need completed starting, chronological age of 24 months, the published official racing age/sex conditions, veterinary clearance, soundness ≥80, hoof health ≥65, fatigue ≤55, compatible registration/location, five days race recovery and available bookings.

## Trial

Actual rules: The starting-plan schooling trial does not count as an official race. Check each racecard’s age, sex, registration, location and booking conditions. No potential-cap or suggested-skill gate.

Suggested relevant skills: **Education only**. Handling, gate acceptance, balance and confidence.

Work: Complete the ten-step starting plan. The controlled schooling trial is education, not an official win.

Next step: Maiden after final readiness clearance.

## Maiden

Actual rules: Normal entries require completed starting and chronological age ≥24 months. At most 0 career wins (unwon horses only). Check each racecard’s age, sex, registration, location and booking conditions. No potential-cap or suggested-skill gate.

Suggested relevant skills: **About 35–55**. Gate 35+, focus 40+, relevant surface 40+; develop speed/acceleration for sprints or stamina/aerobic efficiency for longer races.

Work: Gate schooling, Base conditioning and Surface familiarization.

Next step: After the first win, leave maidens. Try Novice, Allowance/Conditions or an appropriate Claiming race.

## Novice

Actual rules: Normal entries require completed starting and chronological age ≥24 months. At most 2 career wins (published novice restriction). Check each racecard’s age, sex, registration, location and booking conditions. No potential-cap or suggested-skill gate.

Suggested relevant skills: **About 45–60**. Balanced relevant race skills around 45–60; reliable gate response, settling and recovery.

Work: Auto Balanced, Pace exercise and Group schooling.

Next step: Allowance/Conditions, a suitable handicap after a rating is established, or Open Stakes when competitive.

## Claiming

Actual rules: Normal entries require completed starting and chronological age ≥24 months. Check each racecard’s age, sex, registration, location and booking conditions. No potential-cap or suggested-skill gate.

Suggested relevant skills: **About 40–60**. Choose rivals and distance that suit demonstrated ability; soundness and consistency matter more than an expensive pedigree.

Work: Work on the weakest relevant race skill; keep fatigue low.

Next step: A parallel route to allowance races. Claiming is not a mandatory rung. Automatic claiming transfers are not implemented; use Market for purchases.

## Allowance

Actual rules: Normal entries require completed starting and chronological age ≥24 months. Check each racecard’s age, sex, registration, location and booking conditions. No potential-cap or suggested-skill gate.

Suggested relevant skills: **About 50–65**. Speed plus acceleration/kick for sprinting; stamina/aerobic/cruising for distance. Add pace control, traffic and composure.

Work: Sprint work or Extended gallop matched to distance; Pace exercise.

Next step: Handicap or Open Stakes. Consider Listed only after competitive runs against stronger fields.

## Handicap

Actual rules: Normal entries require completed starting and chronological age ≥24 months. Established official rating 35–100; requires at least three rated finishes. Check each racecard’s age, sex, registration, location and booking conditions. No potential-cap or suggested-skill gate.

Suggested relevant skills: **About 50–70**. Pace control, stamina, balance and recovery help under carried weight. Compare official ratings and actual weights.

Work: Pace exercise, Balance work and recovery between starts.

Next step: A parallel ratings/weight route, not a mandatory promotion. Try Open Stakes or Listed when form warrants it.

## Open Stakes

Actual rules: Normal entries require completed starting and chronological age ≥24 months. Check each racecard’s age, sex, registration, location and booking conditions. No potential-cap or suggested-skill gate.

Suggested relevant skills: **About 60–75**. Strong distance/surface-specific skills with tactical awareness, traffic handling, composure and finishing kick.

Work: Group schooling, Late effort and the appropriate sprint/endurance work.

Next step: Listed, then G3 when recent performances support the step up.

## Listed

Actual rules: Normal entries require completed starting and chronological age ≥24 months. Check each racecard’s age, sex, registration, location and booking conditions. No potential-cap or suggested-skill gate.

Suggested relevant skills: **About 65–78**. A well-rounded race profile; aim for 60+ racecraft alongside the horse’s stronger speed/endurance skills.

Work: Target remaining weaknesses rather than train every stat equally.

Next step: G3 / Group 3 after repeated competitive Listed or strong Open performances.

## Grade / Group 3

Actual rules: Normal entries require completed starting and chronological age ≥24 months. Check each racecard’s age, sex, registration, location and booking conditions. No potential-cap or suggested-skill gate.

Suggested relevant skills: **About 70–82**. High relevant athletic skills; reliable gate, composure, pace control and kick. Check rival form and surface fit.

Work: Maintain fitness, refine racecraft and taper before the race.

Next step: G2 / Group 2 when competitive at G3. Step down again if repeatedly outclassed.

## Grade / Group 2

Actual rules: Normal entries require completed starting and chronological age ≥24 months. Check each racecard’s age, sex, registration, location and booking conditions. No potential-cap or suggested-skill gate.

Suggested relevant skills: **About 75–87**. Very strong relevant skills and proven high-level form; recovery and race-day consistency support a sensible campaign.

Work: Maintain strengths, target a clear weakness, allow recovery and plan travel early.

Next step: G1 / Group 1 when recent form, soundness and the likely field justify it.

## Grade / Group 1

Actual rules: Normal entries require completed starting and chronological age ≥24 months. Check each racecard’s age, sex, registration, location and booking conditions. No potential-cap or suggested-skill gate.

Suggested relevant skills: **About 80–95**. Exceptional relevant athletic ability plus advanced racecraft, surface/distance fit, fitness and composure. No skill number guarantees a win.

Work: Maintain a mature horse within its inherited ceilings; avoid exhausting it to chase an unreachable number.

Next step: Top level. Choose suitable G1 events or step down; winning does not increase genetic caps.

A maiden winner cannot return to maidens. An unrated horse needs three rated finishes before handicap eligibility. Do not train an inherently capped horse endlessly to chase these suggestions; move to a suitable distance, surface or competitive level instead.
