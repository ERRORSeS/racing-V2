# Injury balance and repeat travel

Default exposure multipliers are now 12× for races, 6× for training and 2× for turnout. A neutral 1,600m, eight-runner reference is 0.8064% per race; standard training is 0.144% per session and turnout 0.0072% per day. Existing risk factors, frequency setting and 6% / 3.5% / 0.2% caps remain. These are game balance values, not asserted real-world incidence rates.

Leg and fetlock fractures together represent about 5.9% of race/training diagnoses. Conditional fatal-complication probabilities are 35% for leg fracture, 40% for fetlock fracture and 10% for tendon rupture, only with Fatalities enabled. These percentages apply after that diagnosis, not to every start. Existing injuries, identities and results are preserved; competitive performance formulas are unchanged.

Travel now offers voluntary withdrawal of future entries that conflict with the destination or arrival deadline. Entry fees are not refunded, and completed results remain unchanged. The destination defaults to home while abroad. Active journeys prevent incompatible origin-country registrations; auto-registration instead considers the destination. Completed stale booking references are reconciled, and canceling a reserved trip abroad preserves the foreign location. Normal trips must finish travel and acclimatization before the next booking.

Verification: seeded injury calibration, fatality setting and record preservation, repeated instant and normal trips, export/import roundtrips, explicit entry withdrawal, cancellation abroad, stale booking repair and route-aware registration. The browser smoke script exercises four saved journeys and Settings rendering.
