# Stud arrivals, own stallions, Memorial and named horse tabs

One new stud arrives each quarter (90 days) in a rotating NPC farm, including fast
mode. Each has a permanent identity and a generated two-generation pedigree. New
breeding prospects have zero recorded race starts; achievements are never invented.
Their service fees reflect genetic quality; established sires retain fees based on
actual race earnings, Group 1 wins and documented progeny results. Quarterly intake
adds only one sire and six pedigree ancestors, not a whole regenerated NPC world.
The intake marker prevents repeated generation after reloading a quarter.

Stud, Breeding and Freezer show **Your stallions**. Retire a mature colt to stud,
use **Breed to this stallion**, or **Collect five vials**. Own mare × own stallion
has no stud fee; collection has its normal fee and may occur once per stallion per
day. Vials retain the sire identity, owner and actual location. Official TB natural
covering and alternative-registry frozen-dose restrictions remain.

Stud service ends at age 25. Individual natural lifespan is 25–35 chronological
years, determined stably from world seed and horse ID without RNG draws. Monthly
processing handles the scheduled natural end of life, including in fast mode;
injury-related fatalities remain a separate existing mechanism. Historical archive
ancestors never receive new simulated deaths. Current horses reaching a natural
end retain their ID, pedigree, ownership, results and offspring. Entries, active
transport, medical cases and sale listings close. No new horse activities can be
performed on deceased horses. The shared ownership guard enforces this beyond the
interface.

**Horses → Memorial** contains deceased owned horses with read-only profiles,
parent links, lifetime racing results and breeding histories. Normal operation
lists omit deceased horses. Existing deaths also appear there automatically.

On Horses, enter a name and choose **Create tab**. In a live horse profile choose
**Add to tab** and select the named tab. A horse may belong to several tabs;
duplicate membership is prevented. Definitions and memberships are in the normal
save/export. Use **Save career** before leaving.

Validation includes quarterly generation and reload idempotence, real pedigrees
without fabricated starts, quality/fee ordering, own-stallion free covering and
five-vial collection, stable lifespan limits and stud-service withdrawal, retained
Memorial identities/read-only profiles, and named-tab save round trips. The browser
flow collects vials, books own-stallion covering, creates/assigns a tab, opens a
Memorial profile, advances to a quarterly intake, manually saves and reloads.
