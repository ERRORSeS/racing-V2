# V7 lifecycle, exposure and market implementation

Implements the V7 additions in Racing_Game_V7_Complete_Codex_Prompt.pdf §§98–103, plus the subsequent age/progression requests. Existing V1–V6 features remain in place. Source-document instructions were applied under the user's explicit clarification: V7 lifecycle/injury changes are allowed; competitive winner/placing/speed formulas and already recorded outcomes must remain intact.

## Racing lifecycle

- One `racingDurability` profile per horse. The actual first canonical start supplies firstRaceDate and firstRacingAge; an entry or scratch supplies neither. DNF is a genuine starter. Never-raced training does not start the clock.
- Default maximum is 1,800 days (five 360-day years); the shared setting permits 1–5 years. The end is exclusive: no starts on or after debut + maximum duration. Sales, country changes, imports, rest and therapy never reset debut. Increasing a configured cap never permits six years or automatically un-retires a horse.
- Individual latent durability derives from inherited recovery capacity, conformation and deterministic individual variation without consuming the simulation RNG. An uncertain 1–5-year projection is separate from the hard outer cap. Exact values/editing require Creative Mode. An estimate never forces a diagnosis on a birthday.
- On expiry an active horse becomes retired with reason **Racing Career Completed**, future entries are scratched/refunded once, and automatic work/racing stops. Its physical soundness, existing medical history, lifetime results, pedigree and breeding/aftercare identity remain. Owner retirement and veterinary retirement have separate reasons.
- Native clinical condition (`soundness`), recoverable ceiling and irreversible limit remain authoritative; the durability profile does not introduce competing copies of them. Recorded case regions state "unspecified" when no limb side was actually diagnosed.

## Exposure and rehabilitation

One seeded probability roll in the existing relevant exposure pipeline. No random injury is generated by migration, a profile render or a career-window birthday. No pasture roll occurs for a horse with zero turnout. Active medical restrictions continue to block racing regardless of fatigue or longevity.

Factors are bounded: activity/intensity/distance/field demand; age (up to 2×); inherited durability/conformation (0.55–1.8×); cumulative/repetitive load (up to 3.5×); documented clinical susceptibility (up to 3×); recent workload/fatigue (up to 3×); hoof care/surface fit, conditions/tack and conditioning. Probability caps are 6% race, 3.5% training, 0.2% turnout per relevant exposure, including the user-configurable injury-frequency multiplier. Typical healthy exposure is far below those upper caps. Existing injury-type/severity/fatal-complication selection remains unchanged.

Cumulative strain is bounded 0–1000 and does not disappear with rest. Repetitive stress is bounded 0–100 and decays 0.65 points/day normally, 1.8 during planned breaks/therapy. Acute fatigue still follows its existing daily recovery service. Training, races and transport record dated loads; 7-/30-day views show actual recent exposure. A 6–12-month therapy can improve reversible physical condition, recent stress and the viable projection inside the original window; it cannot erase chronic findings, raise genetic athletic caps or grant year six.

## Canonical appraisals and market evidence

`appraisal()` is now a read-only selector over one cached `marketAppraisal.currentValue`. Horse, pedigree, Market, Stud, Dashboard and result-impact panels use it.

- Existing estimates are anchored exactly at migration; constructor/history preparation anchors fresh worlds to the old V6 price scale. Cash, paid sale prices, historical earnings and existing result rows are not recalculated. Invalid/nonpositive estimates receive a documented fallback.
- Every new official result has one processed result ID and one permanent ValuationChanged record. In-race clinical damage is included in that same update. Replays, retries, rendering and save/reload do not apply it again. Older historical results do not mint retroactive valuation transactions.
- Up to ten recent verified results supply class/field-adjusted evidence, top-three rate, rating variation and sample confidence `n/(n+4)`. Context includes actual field size/strength, placing, margin/rating and carried weight. Field-size contribution is sublinear for small fields; ordinary thirds in tiny fields cannot compound an unlimited premium.
- Dimensional evidence maps to existing monetary brackets through an anchored calibration, smoothing and segment ceilings. Ordinary updates cap losses at 8% and gains at 3–12% (depending on performance); a first or repeated high-quality G1 win may have an exceptional bounded increase, and serious clinical updates can have a larger reduction. No default +10% per podium.
- Racing-future demand reaches zero at permanent retirement/window expiry. Credible top-ten graded podium evidence and actual proven offspring can retain breeding demand. Geldings get no stallion premium; fewer-than-four-runner or low-credibility pattern fields cannot manufacture that premium.
- Health/retirement changes, inspection results, actual paid sales and proven offspring use individually keyed material updates. A sale's influence is bounded and non-cash; unlimited-money overpayment does not rebase the market. Existing prices remain historical facts.

Selectors cache only immutable past performance facts. Live age/window, health, condition, ownership and breeding context remain live. Price history is permanent; the profile chart displays the latest 30 recorded events.

## Ages and progression requested during implementation

Every official race type has a **2–4-year-old edition**. Allowance/Conditions and Claiming have repeated rating-free cards at regular meetings, enabling the three actual rated starts needed to establish a handicap rating. Existing adult programs stay available. Special annual classics retain their published age/points conditions. Existing booked/completed age terms are not overwritten.

Every non-Maiden minimum-win progression condition becomes a minimum count of actual top-three finishes. Maiden win restrictions remain actual wins; published maximum-win, rating, medical, registration, rest, travel and classic-point restrictions are separate. Completed historical conditions and placings remain intact. The Races screen has a 2–4 age-group filter and all ten race-type filters.

## Migration, persistence and preservation

Schema 7 accepts schemas 1/2/3/5/6/7 through additive upgrade and existing career-evidence repair. Durability reconstructs only known starts/clinical records; unknown historic injuries stay unknown. Expired legacy careers are retired without rewriting old results or inventing an injury.

A migration is saved once as a full new checkpoint base, even when the source used V6 incremental manifests. This ensures archive horses also receive their new metadata durably. Subsequent writes use the existing revision-checked atomic checkpoint/backup manifests. Durability and appraisal event histories are append-only patch fields. The retained backup remains coherent.

The competitive `resolveRace` implementation is identical to main after removing the new post-settlement bookkeeping call. Changes in racing.js outside that hook are the authorized career-window and top-three eligibility checks. Genetics, athletic development, individual race-form randomness and race-weight calculation modules are unchanged. New lifecycle/medical rules can change future eligible fields or injury-related outcomes, as explicitly approved; old recorded results are preserved.
